Volcano & Coast

Raft the jungle rivers by day, melt into an all-inclusive beach resort by night.

The classic Costa Rica combo. Six days of volcano-country adventure out of one villa, then three days of zero-decisions all-inclusive beach life. The rafting-with-lunch trip you described is the centerpiece.

D1 · Fri 5

Fly into Liberia (LIR) → drive to La Fortuna (2.5h). Check into the villa, evening hot springs.

D2 · Sat 6

La Fortuna Waterfall (early, before crowds) + Mistico hanging bridges + hot springs.

D3 · Sun 7

Sarapiquí River rafting — full day, lunch served on the riverbank. The dream trip.

D4 · Mon 8

Zip lines (Sky Trek) + sloth tour + Arenal volcano hike.

D5 · Tue 9

Drive to the Guanacaste coast (2.5h). Check into the all-inclusive resort. First beach afternoon.

D6 · Wed 10

Catalina Islands snorkeling boat trip.

D7 · Thu 11

Rincón de la Vieja day trip — waterfall hikes, zip lines, hot springs, mud pots.

D8 · Fri 12

Beach day — surf lessons in Tamarindo or a Palo Verde wildlife boat safari.

D9 · Sat 13

Morning beach → fly out of LIR.

Volcano adventures, then a lush green coast where monkeys wake you up.

03

Reef & Ruins

Cave-tube through Mayan underworlds, then snorkel the world's second-largest reef.

The best snorkeling in Central America, plus the single most amazing family adventure in the region — the ATM Cave. Rafting becomes cave tubing (float down a river through limestone caves, lunch stop included). English-speaking, which makes a big group easy.

D1 · Fri 5

Fly into Belize City (BZE) → drive to San Ignacio (2h). Jungle lodge.

D2 · Sat 6

Cave tubing (half day) + Xunantunich Mayan ruins — climb the big temple.

D3 · Sun 7

ATM Cave — full-day underworld adventure, wading through a cave of Mayan artifacts.

D4 · Mon 8

Zip lines (Calico Jack's) + Rio On Pools swimming holes.

D5 · Tue 9

Tikal day trip into Guatemala — jungle temples, howler monkeys, toucans.

D6 · Wed 10

Fly to Ambergris Caye (~1h). Check into the beach resort.

D7 · Thu 11

Hol Chan + Shark Ray Alley snorkeling — nurse sharks and rays, kids go nuts.

D8 · Fri 12

Mexico Rocks snorkeling, pool/beach day, golf-cart exploring.

D9 · Sat 13

Fly out (San Pedro → BZE → home).

04

Raft & Cloud Forest

The rafting-with-lunch dream, world-class zip lines, and cool coffee-country air.

Boquete is the rafting answer — the Rio Caldera run is family-friendly with lunch on the riverbank, and the Chiriquí Viejo is the big one for the adults and older kids. Then a cloud-forest, coffee-farm, waterfall world. The beach leg is the trade-off: good, not great.

D1 · Fri 5

Fly into David (DAV) → drive to Boquete (45 min). Highland lodge.

D2 · Sat 6

Rafting — Rio Caldera (class II–III, family, lunch on the riverbank) for everyone, or Chiriquí Viejo (class III–IV) for the adults.

D3 · Sun 7

D4 · Mon 8

Quetzal Trail cloud-forest hike + Geisha coffee farm tour.

D5 · Tue 9

05

Jungle & Island

A wild riverside eco-lodge, then the most beautiful beach in the Caribbean.

The most adventurous of the five. Pico Bonito is a rainforest eco-lodge with jungle and waterfall hikes, zip lines, and real whitewater on the Cangrejal River. Then Roatán's West Bay — a stunning beach with world-class snorkeling right off the sand. The trade-off: the rafting is class III–IV (too big for the 6–7 year olds) and the logistics are the most complex.

D1 · Fri 5

Fly into San Pedro Sula (SAP) → drive to Pico Bonito (2.5h). Eco-lodge.

D2 · Sat 6

D3 · Sun 7

Cangrejal River rafting (class III–IV) + zip lines.

D4 · Mon 8

Canopy zip lines, river swimming, eco-lodge day.

D5 · Tue 9

Good to know

The fine print that makes a big group trip work.

March weather

March is dry season on the Pacific side (Costa Rica, Panama) and in Belize. Caribbean spots (Bocas, Roatán) are drier too, but can still see showers. Book the Pacific-side trips for guaranteed sun.

Book early

March is peak season. Lock villas and resorts 4–6 months out; book rafting and snorkeling 1–2 months ahead. You're ~7 months out right now — perfect timing.

Group logistics

8–11 people means a 5–6 bedroom villa or 2–3 units, and one 12-seater van or two SUVs. Ask for 4x4 if the villa is up a gravel hill.

Kids & rafting

Class II–III runs (Sarapiquí, Balsa, Rio Caldera, Tenorio) welcome kids as young as 6–7. Class III–IV (Pacuare, Chiriquí Viejo, Cangrejal) usually needs 12+.

Flights

Open-jaw tickets (fly into one city, out of another) avoid backtracking. Costa Rica: SJO/LIR. Belize: BZE. Panama: DAV. Honduras: SAP + RTB.

Prices

Rough per-family lodging estimates: $$ = $2–3k, $$$ = $3–5k, $$$$ = $5–7k for the week. Flights and activities on top.
